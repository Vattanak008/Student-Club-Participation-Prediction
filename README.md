# Project Background

Paragon International University is one of the leading Univerity in Cambodia, Southeast Asia, which was found in 2010, providing youth with many levels of academics including Bachelor, Master, and PhD.

One of the University mission's is to support students' life outside of academic life which result in a new branch of office, the Student Clubs and Cultural Activities Center, which was introduced back in 2015. Since the Covid-19 pandemic, student clubs has suffered from decreased in participation in their events and activity, although the number is recovering. That is why this project is aimed to predict whether if there is sufficient amount of participation for the next academic year of student club.

Insights and recommendation is provided on the the following key areas:
* Participation Predictive Analysis
* Recommendation and Conclusion

# Executive Summary

Using historical data observed from 2025, student club's predicted participants from 2026 is generally more consistent and participant number from 12 clubs out of 19 (63.16%) is forecasted to be more than previous year. As a result, the Student Clubs and Cultural Activities Center have a clear idea on investment plan, specifically on how to distribute budget accordingly to each club.

# Recommendations

* **Budget Allocation**: Distribute more funding to Music Club while decrease significant amount from Afterschool Club as it is predicted to decrease.
* **Event Focus**: Increase event promotion rather than small activity as it is the catalyst of participant generation.
* **Future Request**: As more than half student clubs will increase in participants, it is promoted that the over budget of Student Clubs and Cultural Activities Center to have a budget increase.
